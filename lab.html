Quantum Lab

Build circuits by drag-and-drop or in code, run them on Qiskit Aer, PennyLane, Cirq or qBraid, and watch Bloch spheres, amplitudes and measurement results update live.

The editor understands the common subset of Qiskit, Cirq, PennyLane, qBraid and OpenQASM 2/3 (gates, registers, variables, numpy constants and for loops). Anything beyond that can be run on the Python backend.
