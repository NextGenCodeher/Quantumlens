Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Quantum Lens?

Quantum Lens is an interactive, browser-based toolkit for quantum computing. It allows users to build, visualize, and simulate multi-qubit quantum circuits using a drag-and-drop interface.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Quantum Lens is entirely web-based. You can access the full simulator and all features directly in your browser without any installation.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. Our Tutorials section provides step-by-step guides, and the intuitive visual interface (including the Bloch Sphere) is designed to make complex quantum concepts accessible to newcomers.

What kind of quantum gates are supported?

We support a wide range of standard quantum gates, including Pauli gates (X, Y, Z), Hadamard (H), Phase gates (S, T), Controlled gates (CNOT, CCX), and various rotation gates.

Can I export my quantum circuits?

Yes. Circuits built in the simulator can be exported into standard formats, including OpenQASM, allowing you to run them on real quantum hardware or other simulators.

Is Quantum Lens free to use?

The core simulation and visualization tools are completely free to use.

Which quantum frameworks can I use?

The Quantum Lab reads and writes Qiskit, Cirq, PennyLane, qBraid and OpenQASM, and runs circuits on Qiskit Aer, PennyLane, Cirq and qBraid through the optional Python backend — or instantly in your browser.

What can the AI Quantum Tutor do?

It explains concepts, walks through your circuit step by step, generates algorithm code, finds errors in your code, suggests optimisations and recommends what to learn next based on your progress.
