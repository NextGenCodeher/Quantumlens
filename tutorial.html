Density Matrices & Partial Tracing

When simulating multi-qubit systems, we often need to look at a single qubit's state. This is where Density Matrices (ρ) come in.

Partial Tracing: This operation allows us to "trace out" the environment or other qubits to see the reduced state of a specific qubit. This is vital for understanding how entanglement affects individual measurements.

Visualizing with the Bloch Sphere

The Bloch Sphere provides a 3D visual for single-qubit states. In Quantum Lens, you can see these update in real-time as you drag and drop gates.

  • North Pole: State |0⟩
  • South Pole: State |1⟩
  • Center: Maximally mixed state

How to Use the Simulator

Our simulator supports two modes of input:

Gate-Based: Simply drag icons onto the circuit lines. Perfect for beginners.

Code-Based: Enter QASM or Qiskit-style code for complex algorithmic design.

Both modes are also built right into Quantum Lens: the Quantum Lab combines a drag-and-drop builder with a code lab for Qiskit, Cirq, PennyLane, qBraid and OpenQASM.
